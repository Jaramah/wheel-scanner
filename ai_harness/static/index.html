Choose how to power your chats

Both paths end in the same chat — they differ only in who pays Anthropic.

Bring your own key

Use your Anthropic API account (console.anthropic.com). Usage bills to you directly, pay-as-you-go. The key is encrypted server-side and never returns to the browser.

Subscribe to a plan

No key needed — chat on the app's account with a monthly allowance. Billing stub: plans activate instantly, free, for testing.
